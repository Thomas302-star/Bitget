01 / OVERVIEW

One place to understand the basics.

A centralized exchange is a platform that can provide access to different crypto related products and account tools through one interface. The exact products, availability, fees, eligibility and regional access can change.

This page is for general education only. It is not financial advice and does not recommend buying, selling or trading any asset.

03 / SERVICES

Understand the main platform services.

Different services serve different purposes. Use this section as a simple guide to the categories you may encounter on a centralized crypto platform.

01

Buy & Sell Crypto

Exchange platforms can provide tools for purchasing or selling supported digital assets. Methods, fees and availability can vary.

02

P2P

Peer to peer marketplaces connect users who want to exchange assets using supported payment methods.

03

Deposits & Withdrawals

Account funding and asset transfers are common exchange functions, with supported networks and requirements varying by asset.

04

Spot

Spot markets allow users to exchange supported assets at market prices, subject to the platform's rules and risks.

05

Futures & Derivatives

Derivatives are products whose value is linked to an underlying asset. They can involve substantially higher risk and complexity.

06

Rewards & Programs

Platforms may offer campaigns, rewards or other programs from time to time. Terms and eligibility can change.

04 / TRADING

Understand the difference between trading markets.

Trading products are not interchangeable. Each market has its own structure, pricing mechanics and risk profile.

KEY IDEAHigher complexity can mean higher risk.

Before using any trading product, understand how it works, what can cause losses and whether it is available in your region.

01

Spot Trading

Spot markets involve exchanging supported assets at the current market price. The asset itself is the focus of the transaction.
