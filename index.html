07 / FAQ

Common questions, clearly answered.

A few simple answers to help you understand the platform categories covered on this page.

?
What is Bitget?

Bitget is a centralized crypto exchange platform. The products and services available can vary by region, eligibility and current platform terms.

What is a centralized crypto exchange?

It is a platform that can provide users with access to crypto markets and account related tools through a centralized service.

What is the difference between spot and futures?

Spot markets involve the direct exchange of supported assets. Futures involve contracts linked to an underlying asset and can introduce additional complexity and risk.

Are all Bitget products available everywhere?

No. Product availability, eligibility, restrictions and requirements can depend on location, product type and current platform rules.

Are crypto products risk free?

No. Crypto assets and related financial products can involve significant risk, including the possibility of losing money. Information on this page is educational and not financial advice.
